NoLauncher

A calm Android home screen. No icons, no badges, no dots — just names, and limits that hold.

Download APKSource code

Android 8.0 or newer · free · open source

What it does

  • Home — up to 7 essential apps as plain text on black.
  • Side panels — swipe left or right for two short lists of important apps.
  • Search & dock — search apps, or open a small dock of monochrome icons.
  • All apps — swipe up for an A–Z list, swipe sideways through groups.
  • Time limits — daily minutes per app; at zero the app closes. Stricter limits start now, looser ones tomorrow.
  • Who's waiting — swipe down to see which apps have notifications. Names only, never content.
  • Rearrange — drag apps into the order you want on home, the dock and the panels.

Install

  1. Download the latest NoLauncher-v*.apk from Releases and open it.
  2. If Play Protect blocks it, install from a computer instead: adb install -r NoLauncher-v*.apk.
  3. Open NoLauncher → Get started and choose it as your default Home app.
  4. Pick your essentials, dock and panels. Settings are under ⚙ in the dock.
  5. Android 13+: if Accessibility or Notification access is greyed out, open Settings → Apps → NoLauncher → ⋮ → Allow restricted settings.

Privacy

NoLauncher works entirely on your phone. It has no internet permission, collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere. Privacy policy · Terms of service
