Privacy policy

NoLauncher · Effective 22 November 2026

NoLauncher works entirely on your phone. It has no internet permission, collects nothing and sends nothing anywhere.

What the app uses

  • Your installed apps — to list, search and open them.
  • The app in front (its package name only) — to count minutes for the time limits you set.
  • Which apps have notifications — the app name, count and time, for the “who's waiting” screen. Notification text is never read or stored.

Permissions

  • Accessibility service — detects which app is in the foreground so time limits can warn you and close the app. It does not read screen content or what you type.
  • Notification access — counts notifications per app. Content is ignored.
  • Do Not Disturb access — lets the Focus button turn Do Not Disturb on and off.
  • Request uninstall — opens Android's uninstall screen when you choose Uninstall.

What we collect

Nothing. NoLauncher has no account, no analytics, no ads and no crash reporting. The developer receives no data from the app. Everything it saves (your layout, lists, notes and settings) stays in the app's private storage on your device and is deleted when you uninstall it.

Third parties

None. When you search the web from NoLauncher, your browser or search app opens and its own privacy policy applies.

Children

The app is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page with a new effective date.
